# Customer Segmentation & LTV

Most marketing teams know who their best customers are. Few can put a number on what each one is worth.

## The problem
A retailer sends the same newsletter to everyone. The loyal regular who orders every month gets the same email as someone who bought once, two years ago. Customers who are slowly drifting away go unnoticed until they're gone, and discounts end up with people who would have bought anyway.

## What I did
I took raw transaction data and turned it into a customer strategy. Each customer was scored on how recently they bought, how often, and how much they spend (RFM). Clustering then sorted them into natural segments, and I estimated what each segment is worth over time (LTV). Every segment ends with a clear next move.

## What's inside
- Data cleaning, with every removal documented
- Segment profiles: size, share of revenue, lifetime value
- A one-page playbook: who to keep, who to win back, who to leave alone
